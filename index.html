Coffin of the Seven Sins — Full Details

Murdered. Buried. Reborn with a death list.

What it's about

The most beloved princess of the Da Yin Dynasty dies under mysterious circumstances — and wakes inside her own coffin on the day of her funeral. Reborn with the "Coffin of the Seven Sins," a System that exposes the sin in every soul, Su Lingxi discovers she was never sick. She was cursed. Murdered. And the men who killed her are standing over her grave.

Armed with a list of seven names — each bound to one of the Seven Deadly Sins — she rises to reclaim her life, and to collect, one by one, the offerings she is owed.

Key Selling Points

  • Rebirth revenge with a quantified death list (LitRPG-style hook)
  • Dark, opulent "beautiful corpse, vicious queen" aesthetic
  • Western Seven Sins × Eastern palace intrigue crossover
  • Strong female lead, high-stakes political thriller

Format & Licensing

  • Medium: anime short-drama / motion-comic (1–3 min episodes)
  • Season 1: 10 episodes (outline + bible + Episode 1 sample available)
  • License: full-season, per-episode, or custom adaptation
  • Deliverables: Story Bible + Episode Guide + Sample Script
